Hammam glossary

Every hammam has its own vocabulary.

Some of what happens at Arya Spa is a lot older than the building it's in.

Tas · Hammam bowl

A vessel three thousand years old.

The hammam bowl, used to scoop water from the basin. First used in Anatolia in the Phrygian period, it also appears in the sarcophagi of the Lydians and the Roman Empire. According to a long-forgotten belief, the domed rise at its centre stands for the navel of the goddess Cybele.

Kurna · Hammam basin

The stone beneath the taps.

The marble or stone basin set beneath the taps. Hot and cold water are mixed here to the desired temperature, then scooped with the bowl and poured over the body. Some of the finest stonework in Ottoman hammams appears on the surfaces and edges of the basins.
