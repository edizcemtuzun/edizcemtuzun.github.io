ErizoGastro & Bar

erizo (Sp.): hedgehog

Anatolian cooking, over today's fire.

Erizo's kitchen does not try to preserve Anatolian flavours so much as reread them. Vegetables, herbs and fruit are picked by hand at the farm each morning and cooked over fire, simply and patiently. The menu changes with the season.

Dinner is served outdoors, under the stars. The kitchen is led by chef Yüksel Demirkıran.
