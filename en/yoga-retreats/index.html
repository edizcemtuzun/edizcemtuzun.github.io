Daily practice

A wooden floor, an open window, birdsong.

The shala stands among the trees; with its wooden walls and floor-to-ceiling glass it feels like part of the garden. Mats, blocks and cushions are provided. Besides the morning classes, there is also practice on the beach before sunrise.
