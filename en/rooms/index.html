Olives Breakfast

Breakfast under the trees.

Olives is a few steps from the entrance. Turkish breakfast and a few dishes from elsewhere are made to order; vegetables and herbs arrive early from Huysuz Çoban, bread from the stone oven, jams are made in-house. Cats and hens wander nearby.
