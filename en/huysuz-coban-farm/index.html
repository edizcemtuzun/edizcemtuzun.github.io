Kimera's kitchen starts on a farm. Huysuz Çoban is a working farm ten minutes from the hotel, worked without chemicals and at the pace the soil allows.

  • ProduceVegetables, herbs, dairy and preserves
  • MethodNo chemicals; picked when ready
From farm to table

Picked early, on the table the same day.

Tomatoes, peppers and herbs are picked in the first light. The farm's produce is used in every kitchen of the hotel from breakfast to dinner: goat's cheese and eggs beside stone-oven bread at Olives, vegetables for the fire at Erizo.

Daily life

From the coop to the jar.

The hens roam the farm freely; their eggs go from the coop to breakfast at Olives. The goats are out in the fields. What the season leaves over goes into jars: kumquat jam and preserves.
